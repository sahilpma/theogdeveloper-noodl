Built differently, on purpose

We're not that app.

Most productivity apps are designed around consistency — streaks, points, badges. For a lot of people that's motivating. For ADHD brains, it often becomes another system to fail at.

Noodl is designed around recovery, not consistency. Miss a day? Your app looks exactly the same as yesterday. No guilt. No fire. Just your tasks, still there, whenever you're ready.

How it works

Three things. That's it.

1

Capture

Tap the button, type what's in your head, done. No fields to fill, no project to assign.

2

Focus

Pick a task (or skip that part), start the timer, and work. Jot thoughts without breaking flow.

3

Rest

Close the app. Tomorrow, unfinished tasks wait in Plan, parked — quietly, without drama.
